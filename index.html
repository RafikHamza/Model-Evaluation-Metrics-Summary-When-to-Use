3/9

Confusion Matrix

Predicted +Predicted -
Actual +True Positive (TP)False Negative (FN)
Actual -False Positive (FP)True Negative (TN)
  • TP: Predicted yes, actually yes
  • FP: Predicted yes, actually no
  • FN: Predicted no, actually yes
  • TN: Predicted no, actually no
Example: TP=30, FP=20, FN=10, TN=40 for a test on 100 patients.

Hypothesis Testing

Null Hypothesis (H0): No association between variables.

Alternative Hypothesis (HA): Variables are associated.

5/9

Chi-Square Test Examples

  1. Gender vs Preference: Tea vs Coffee survey.
  2. Ad Campaign: Click vs No-Click by Age group.
  3. Device Type: Purchase vs No-Purchase by Device.
6/9

Chi-Square Test & p-Value

χ²: Σ (O–E)²/E compares observed vs expected counts.

df: (rows–1)×(cols–1)

p-value: Probability of χ² under null. If <0.05, reject null (variables linked).

7/9

How to Choose Metrics

  • Problem type: regression vs classification.
  • Error cost: FNs vs FPs.
  • Report at least two metrics for clarity.
  • Use statistical tests (p-values, χ²) when needed.
